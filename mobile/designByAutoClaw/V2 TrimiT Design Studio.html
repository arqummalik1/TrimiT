What's redesigned in V2

  • 5 live themes. Verdant (recommended — the unclaimed wellness hue), Oxblood heritage red, Plum Velvet, Rosewater, and Kiln (your current terracotta, refined). Every text & button pair passes WCAG-AA contrast in both modes.
  • True light / dark. Light mode is a genuinely light room; dark mode is genuinely dark — both tinted in each theme's hue family, never pure black or white.
  • Compact home. Editorial hero → snap-scroll rail of small cards → bento categories → scannable list rows with live "Next 4:30 PM" availability chips (Fresha's conversion pattern).
  • Floating glass dock. iOS-26-style translucent pill nav with a sliding active indicator — replaces the classic tab bar.
  • Uber-Eats-style cart. + buttons morph into checks, a glass summary bar springs up — services grouped in collapsible menus instead of giant cards.
  • Slot picking with ceremony. Day chips with availability dots, grouped slot pills, springy select with rotating check, live draining hold-timer (calm → amber → red).
  • Trust inline. "Pay at salon" and "Free cancellation" chips ride along the whole flow — the anti-prepay pitch that wins in India.
  • Typography carries the brand. Oversized Cormorant Garamond italics × tight Manrope — the fashion-editorial move your fonts already support.
